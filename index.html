Scientist | Quantum Magnetic Sensors | Light–Matter Interaction

I am a physics enthusiast with a strong interest in quantum sensing, atomic magnetometry, exciton–polaritons, Raman spectroscopy, and two-dimensional materials. My current work focuses on developing product-oriented quantum magnetic sensors based on vapor-cell magnetometry. During my doctoral research, I studied light–matter interaction in anisotropic 2D-material microcavities, with emphasis on exciton–polaritons and polarization-dependent Raman processes.

About

I completed my Ph.D. in Physics from the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur. My research background includes resonance Raman scattering, anisotropic excitons, optical microcavities, exciton–polariton physics, and polarization-resolved spectroscopy. I am currently working as a Scientist at Nostradamus Technologies Private Limited (QuBeats), where I lead research and development activities on quantum magnetic sensors.

I am particularly interested in building physics-based technologies that move from laboratory demonstrations toward robust real-world sensing platforms.
